Simply download, unzip, and run!

## Features

You can do a lot with Bean, such as:

- Play: Launch an endless runner game, controlling Bean to get over the rocks! (Increases her mood the more you score!)

- Feed: Give Bean some food, yum! (Increases Hunger)

- Sleep: Naptime! Cats need their rest, so you can tell Bean to get some! Bean will also automatically rest if she's too tired. (Increases Energy)

- Throw: Throw Bean around and hear the boings as she hits the edge of the screen and custom windows!

- Pet: Click on Bean to pet her! (Increases mood)

## Who IS Bean?

Bean is my robotics team shop cat that roams around the shop. She loves to jump on the table, lay down on everyone's keyboards, and receive pets. One time, she sent a message to our team Slack that was just nonsense. Woops. Everyone on the team loves her, and our robot is usually named after her in some way! Our 2025 Robot was named Black Beard, and in 2026 it was named Black Fang!
